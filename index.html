QUESTIONS?

Good to
know.

Is this a real entertainment center?

PLAYZONE is a concept website and portfolio demonstration. Activities, prices and events are examples.

Will I be charged when I book?

No. Payments are not connected. Visit plans are saved only in your browser.

Will I get a ticket by email?

No. You can view and print your saved plan here. No email is sent.

Can I make a booking from another device?

Plans stay in the browser where you save them. Availability is illustrative and is not connected to a venue.
