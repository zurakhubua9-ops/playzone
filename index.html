QUESTIONS?

Good to
know.

Is this a real entertainment center?

PLAYZONE is a concept website and portfolio demonstration. Activities, prices and events are examples.

Will I be charged when I book?

No. Payments are not connected. Demo bookings are saved only in your browser.

Will I get a ticket by email?

No. You can view and print a demo ticket locally. Email delivery requires a secure backend.

Can I make a booking from another device?

This prototype does not sync between devices. Live multi-user availability requires server-side reservations.
